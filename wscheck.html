Can this browser reach the Zeigote server?

On the host's PC: start the server (start-zeigote-server.bat), keep ws://127.0.0.1:9080 and press Test. The first time, the browser may ask to let this site "access other apps and services on this device". Choose Allow.
Friends: paste the wss://….trycloudflare.com address (or the whole friend link) and press Test. No prompt is expected.
